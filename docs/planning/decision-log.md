# Decision Log

Authoritative record of product and architecture decisions. Append-only: a reversed decision gets a new entry that references the old ID; the old entry is never edited except to add `Superseded by D-xxx`.

Format: ID, date, decision, why (short), status. Longer reasoning lives in the doc that owns the topic.

| ID | Date | Decision | Why | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-001 | 2026-06-01 | Product is an **adaptive coaching system built on expert-designed training frameworks**, not an "AI generates a plan" app. | AI-generated plans are trivially copied (ChatGPT does it). A progression engine plus adaptive feedback loop is defensible. | Active |
| D-002 | 2026-06-01 | Primary users: first-time dog owners and new puppy owners. Secondary: owners with common obedience challenges. Advanced behavior cases are a future segment. | Puppy owners need guidance immediately, are motivated, and are a large market. Obedience issues are a problem state inside those segments, not a separate segment. | Active |
| D-003 | 2026-06-01 | MVP skill scope: Puppy Foundations, Potty, Basic Obedience (sit/down/stay), Recall, Leash Walking, Jumping, Excessive Barking, Home-Alone Confidence, Trick Training. Socialization is handled as an age-windowed priority item. | Covers the puppy-owner job-to-be-done without touching safety-sensitive behavior modification. | Active |
| D-004 | 2026-06-01 | **Excluded from MVP and routed to professional help**: human or dog aggression, resource guarding, bite history, severe anxiety, cases needing veterinary behavioral intervention. "Home-alone confidence" is in scope; clinical separation anxiety is not. | Bad advice on "sit" annoys a user; bad advice on aggression gets someone bitten. | Active |
| D-005 | 2026-06-01 | Business model: freemium subscription. Free covers profile, assessment, personalized onboarding, and limited daily training; premium unlocks full adaptive coaching and progression. No one-time purchase. | The value is ongoing adaptation, not the initial plan. Users must be able to try the loop before paying. | Active |
| D-006 | 2026-06-01 | Launch scope: United States only, English only. | Simpler legal/disclaimer language, support, and content. Architecture must not block later expansion. | Active |
| D-007 | 2026-06-01 | AI does not invent training methodology. Methodology comes from expert frameworks; AI handles personalization, progression explanation, adaptation communication, coaching, and scheduling language. | Consistency, safety, auditability. "Frameworks = curriculum, AI = tutor." | Active |
| D-008 | 2026-06-01 | Training philosophy: **positive-reinforcement first**. The app teaches reward timing, markers, reinforcement schedules, management, environment setup, and motivation. It MUST NOT recommend aversive tools (prong, e-collar, slip-lead corrections). | Fewest business, liability, app-store, and partnership risks. Easiest for inexperienced owners. Not a claim that it is the only valid methodology. | Active |
| D-009 | 2026-06-01 | The system generates only the **next ~3 days** of training, regenerated from feedback. It never produces a fixed multi-week plan. | A 12-week plan is wrong by week 3. The product answers "what should I do with my dog tomorrow?" continuously. | Active |
| D-010 | 2026-06-01 | Users train **multiple goals simultaneously**; the engine manages a portfolio of skills with priority, progress, and difficulty, and actively caps workload. | Real dog training is not linear; a puppy learns many things at once. Unlimited goals create overwhelm, so the engine limits. | Active |
| D-011 | 2026-06-01 | After a session the AI gives **immediate coaching feedback** and the next session adapts. Silent adaptation rejected. | Silent adaptation feels like a planner; feedback feels like a coach. | Active |
| D-012 | 2026-06-01 | Session feedback is three-tier: required (completed yes/no, rating 1–5), optional notes, adaptive AI follow-up question only when it would improve coaching. | Feedback must take under 30 seconds or users stop logging and adaptation dies. | Active |
| D-013 | 2026-06-01 | The home screen is **Today's Training**, not a dashboard. The output is called an *agenda*, not a *plan*. | Users ask "what do I do today?", not "show me my stats." "Plan" implies a fixed roadmap. | Active |
| D-014 | 2026-06-01 | Onboarding ends with a **"Meet Your Dog's Training Path"** screen that explains what will be prioritized and why before Day 1. | Users need evidence the system understood their dog before trusting its recommendations. This is the first "wow" moment. | Active |
| D-015 | 2026-06-01 | Every exercise MUST include goal, setup, step-by-step instructions, duration or repetitions, success criteria, common mistakes, and troubleshooting, written for an owner who has never trained a dog. | Vague exercises push the work back onto the user. Specificity is the product. | Active |
| D-016 | 2026-06-01 | Progression is modeled as **skill trees with stages**; advancement, maintenance, and simplification are decided by thresholds on recent performance. Progression methodology (when to advance) is separate from training methodology (how to teach). | Progression logic is the actual intelligence and the moat. | Active |
| D-017 | 2026-06-01 | Skill selection uses a deterministic **priority formula**: Safety + Window + Weakness + UserGoal + Decay − Overload. All weights and thresholds are tunable configuration, never hardcoded. | Starting weights are hypotheses; they must be calibrated against real cohorts without rebuilding. | Active |
| D-018 | 2026-06-01 | The system models the **owner's skill tier** (first-time, intermediate, experienced) as a prior that adjusts thresholds, step size, concurrent skills, and coaching depth, and refines the tier from outcomes. | Training failure is frequently owner-execution failure; the engine must disambiguate dog-side, owner-side, and biology-side causes. | Active |
| D-019 | 2026-06-01 | A **confidence score** (0–100) gates advancement internally. It is **not shown to users** in MVP; users see plain-language status. | Success rate alone cannot distinguish fluent from brittle behavior. Users do not know what "Confidence 78" means. | Active |
| D-020 | 2026-06-01 | Architecture is a **rules-driven adaptive platform with AI augmentation**. Pipeline: Frameworks → Progression Engine → Priority Engine → Adaptive Rules → Training State → AI Coaching Layer → User. Stage advancement, priority, confidence, progression, and safety decisions are deterministic and never delegated to an LLM. | Testable, explainable, auditable, cheap, consistent. AI must never be the source of truth. | Active |
| D-021 | 2026-06-01 | AI is limited to four jobs: dynamic questionnaire follow-ups, coaching responses, exercise personalization (structured template to natural language), and notes interpretation (free text to structured signals). | Keeps LLM calls bounded, cheap, and replaceable. | Active |
| D-022 | 2026-06-01 | Every recommendation carries a machine-readable **reason object** (skill, stage, success rate, confidence, priority, action). The AI explains the reason; it does not generate it. | Explainability to users and to ourselves when tuning. | Active |
| D-023 | 2026-06-01 | Training frameworks are **structured domain data** (metadata, stages, milestones, exercises, advancement rules, regression rules, coaching guidance), not prose content. Prose instructions are generated from the structure. | Changing a stage distance from 15 ft to 12 ft must be a data edit, not a code change. | Active |
| D-024 | 2026-06-01 | Framework authoring for MVP: **simple admin panel + versioned framework definitions**. A dog is **locked to the framework version** it started on. CMS-style editor is Phase 2; trainer authoring platform Phase 3+. | Versioning is cheap on day one and painful to retrofit. Locking prevents mid-training progression changes. | Active |
| D-025 | 2026-06-01 | Media (photo and video) analysis is **out of MVP scope**. Photos are a Phase 3 candidate, video coaching Phase 4. | Large infra and AI cost before the core loop is validated. Long term it is a key differentiator. | Active |
| D-026 | 2026-10-20 | Platform: **mobile-first responsive web app, installable as a PWA** with web push. Native apps deferred until the core loop is validated. | One codebase, no app-store review cycle, fastest path to validating the hypothesis. | Active |
| D-027 | 2026-10-20 | Stack: **Next.js (App Router) + Supabase (Postgres, Auth, RLS, Storage) + Vercel**. LLM calls via Vercel AI SDK through AI Gateway with Claude as the default model family. | Relational data fits skills, progress, and sessions; Supabase provides auth and RLS out of the box; Vercel is the natural Next.js host. | Active |
| D-028 | 2026-10-20 | Coding agent target is **Claude Code**. `AGENTS.md` is the universal source of truth, `RULES.md` holds non-negotiables, `CLAUDE.md` points at both with Claude Code specifics. | Kit's tooling. AGENTS.md keeps other agents viable. | Active |
| D-029 | 2026-10-20 | Product name: **Biscuit**. Working title "Adaptive Dog Training Coach" retired. A US trademark search is a launch gate (Biscuit Pet Care exists in the UK in an adjacent category). | The biscuit is the reward; the name states the methodology and reads as "dog" instantly. | Active |
| D-030 | 2026-10-20 | Training frameworks will be rebuilt from **deep, cited research** per skill. Sequence: engine spec and framework schema first (Sessions 1–2), then research fills the schema. Founder-authored v0 frameworks are the shape reference until then. Review by at least one credentialed positive-reinforcement trainer is a launch gate. | Research is only useful if it fills the fields the engine consumes. Trainer review is cheap liability insurance before charging money. | Active |
| D-031 | 2026-10-20 | Documentation style: **normative body** (MUST, SHOULD, MAY, IDs, tables) with rationale confined to a **Why** footer per section. | A coding agent needs unambiguous requirements; humans still need the reasoning, quarantined so it is never mistaken for a requirement. | Active |
| D-032 | 2026-10-20 | Git workflow is fully automated by the agent: branch per unit of work, as many commits as needed, push, open a PR, squash-merge, delete branch. Applies to docs now and code later. Main is never force-pushed. | Kit reviews content in conversation; the PR is a record, not a gate. | Active |
| D-033 | 2026-10-20 | The required Tier 1 feedback question is **defined by the framework's measurement type**, not a global star rating: cue skills ask successes out of the exercise's N tries; interval skills use proportion chips; management tracks ask their own count; exposure checklists ask the dog's reaction. The engine always receives a success proportion plus a precision flag. | A 1–5 star is a feeling; thresholds are rates. One widget cannot serve "out of ten" and "accidents today" at once. Resolves R-06. | Active |
| D-034 | 2026-10-20 | Confidence keeps its latency term but MVP config sets its weight to **0** and renormalizes the others (success 0.41, consistency 0.24, environments 0.24, recency 0.11). | No MVP signal measures response speed; a neutral default only dilutes. Enabling later is a config publish. | Active |
| D-035 | 2026-10-20 | **Puppy Foundations is a bundle** of small independent skills (name, marker, handling, bite inhibition, impulse control) grouped under one user-facing label; the engine scores and progresses each separately with a per-group concurrency cap. | Components progress independently; one skill with many stages would force a false order and let one bad component drag the track. | Active |
| D-036 | 2026-10-20 | A day's agenda is an **ordered list of mini-sessions with no clock times**, plus a routine strip for continuous tracks (potty). One optional reminder time. | Fewest assumptions about the owner's day; no rescheduling logic; missing "morning" cannot make the owner feel behind. | Active |
| D-037 | 2026-10-20 | When a slot frees, the engine **activates the next deferred goal automatically**, the coaching message announces it, and the owner may swap it for another deferred goal within a short window. | Removes the "what next?" decision the product exists to remove, without silent changes (D-011). | Active |
| D-038 | 2026-10-20 | Owner control over today's agenda: any order, skip with reason, an optional too-hard / too-easy tap in feedback. **No editing of exercise parameters or stages.** | Parameter edits destroy the progression signal; the difficulty tap is the sanctioned channel for owner influence. | Active |
| D-039 | 2026-10-20 | **Comeback mode** after a gap of 5+ days: lighter first day, confidence decayed by recency, advancement frozen until one fresh counted session re-verifies the level; after 14+ days advancing skills drop one stage. | Dogs regress during gaps; "pick up where you left off" would misread the first session back as a simplify trigger; a reassessment questionnaire adds friction at the highest-churn moment. | Active |
