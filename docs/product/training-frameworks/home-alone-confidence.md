# Home-Alone Confidence

Status: **scope brief** for the research session (D-030). No stage data yet.

## Scope

In scope: teaching a dog to be calm and settled when alone for normal household durations, including crate comfort and gradual absence building for a dog that is mildly uneasy (whining briefly, following the owner, settling slowly).

Out of scope (D-004): clinical separation anxiety: self-injury, destruction, panic, elimination from distress, sustained vocalization. The screening questions MUST route these to professional and veterinary help before this skill is offered.

## What the research session must produce

1. Stages for crate/confinement comfort (equipment comfort → short closed-door durations → absences with the owner out of sight → leaving the house) with duration targets and the ping-pong rule for absence lengths.
2. Measurement: calm indicators the owner can report (settles within N minutes, no vocalization, relaxed on return) and what counts as a failed trial.
3. Advancement/regression criteria and the explicit rule that duration increases are not linear (random short absences interleaved).
4. Biology/age notes: puppy bladder limits bound absence durations; adolescence effects.
5. Coaching guidance: departure and arrival rituals, enrichment, common owner mistakes (dramatic goodbyes, punishing on return).
6. Red-flag indicators that must trigger escalation mid-training.
7. Cited sources.
