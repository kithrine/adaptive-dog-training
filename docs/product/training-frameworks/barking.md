# Excessive Barking

Status: **v0** founder draft. Measurement: management track (reduction from baseline by bark type; "quiet" cue reliability). Category: common challenge. Safety weight: 0.2. Minimum age: 8 weeks.

## Principles

- Barking has types with different protocols: alert, attention-seeking, boredom, reactivity, separation-related. The first stage is identifying the type from context.
- Attention-seeking barks lose reinforcement; alert barks are acknowledged then redirected; no punishment for barking, mark and reward cessation.
- Reactive barking at threshold distance uses desensitization at a distance where the dog can still focus; never flooding.
- Severe reactivity or separation-related barking with distress escalates to the safety flow (D-004).

## Stages

| # | Name | Environment | Advance when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identify triggers | home, logging | trigger map complete: 3+ contexts logged with type |
| 2 | Reinforcement break | home | attention-seeking barking reduced ≥ 50% from baseline |
| 3 | Stimulus control | home, then outdoor | "quiet" cue reliable ≥ 80% in low-distraction environment |

## Milestones

| Milestone | Stage | Confidence required |
|---|---|---|
| Know why the dog barks | 1 | 70 |
| Quiet for attention | 2 | 75 |
| Quiet on cue | 3 | 80 |

## Exercise template (Stage 3 example)

- Parameters: reps 8–10, environment low distraction indoors.
- Setup: a mild known trigger (doorbell recording at low volume), treats.
- Steps: trigger; allow 1–2 barks; say "quiet" once; the instant the dog pauses, mark and reward; gradually increase quiet duration before reward.
- Success: dog stops within 2 seconds of cue in 8 of 10 reps.
- Common mistakes: shouting over the dog (joins the barking); rewarding during barking; raising trigger intensity too fast.
- If struggling: lower trigger volume; reward earlier pauses.

## Coaching guidance

| Problem | Response |
|---|---|
| Barks at window at passersby | Management (block view) plus Stage 2 redirect; reward looking at owner |
| Barks when ignored | Attention-seeking: zero reinforcement, reward quiet, enrichment for boredom |
| Lunging and barking at dogs on walks | Reactivity: keep below threshold distance; if intense or escalating, route to professional (safety flow) |
| Owner execution check | Is any family member responding to attention barks? Trigger intensity controlled? |
