# Potty Training (House Training)

Status: **v0** founder draft. Measurement: management track (accidents and signaling). Category: foundation. Safety weight: 0.4. Minimum age: 8 weeks. Typical duration: 8 weeks to 6 months depending on breed and age.

## Principles

- Governed by **bladder physiology** as much as behavior: a puppy cannot reliably hold longer than roughly age-in-months + 1 hour. Stages 3–4 are biology-gated (PM-030).
- Prevention and schedule beat correction. Accidents are information, never punished.
- Reward outdoor elimination immediately, at the spot.

## Stages

| # | Name | Age guide | Interval | Supervision | Advance when | Biology gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Constant supervision | 8–10 wks | out every 45–60 min awake; every 3–4 h overnight | no indoor freedom; crate when unsupervised | no indoor accidents for 3 consecutive days | none |
| 2 | Scheduled intervals | 10–12 wks | every 90 min awake | supervised only | signals (door, whining, circling) observed 3+ times, or no accidents 5 days | none |
| 3 | Extended hold | 12–16 wks | every 2 h; 3–4 h holds; reduce overnight trips | supervised or gated | no accidents 1 week and consistent signaling | min age ~12 wks |
| 4 | Household freedom | 4–6 mo | 4–6 h holds | supervised zones, expanding | zero accidents for 2+ weeks; accidents now flag regression | min age ~16–20 wks |

## Milestones

| Milestone | Stage | Confidence required |
|---|---|---|
| Accident-free 3 days | 1 | 70 |
| Reliable signaling | 2 | 75 |
| Overnight dry | 3 | 75 |
| House trained | 4 | 80 |

## Exercise templates

Potty exercises are schedule and routine items rather than rep-based drills: timed outings, post-meal and post-nap outings, reward-at-the-spot, crate rest periods, supervision rules, accident clean-up protocol (enzymatic cleaner, no scolding). The agenda surfaces them as a continuous daily track (PM-049) rather than a session.

## Coaching guidance

| Problem | Response |
|---|---|
| Accident shortly after coming in | Stay out longer; reward only after elimination; return outside if nothing happened |
| Accidents at a consistent time | Adjust schedule to precede that time |
| Failing Stage 3 holds under 16 weeks | Biological, not behavioral: coaching says so; return interval to Stage 2 |
| Regression after Stage 4 | Check health (UTI), stress, routine change; return to Stage 3 schedule briefly |
| Owner execution check | Is the puppy supervised 100% indoors? Rewarding at the spot or at the door? Cleaning with enzymatic cleaner? |
