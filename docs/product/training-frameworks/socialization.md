# Socialization

Status: **scope brief** for the research session (D-030). No stage data yet.

## Scope

Not a cue skill: structured, positive exposure to people, dogs, surfaces, sounds, handling, objects, and environments during the critical window (~3–14 weeks, with tapering value to ~16–20 weeks) and controlled continuation afterward. It is the skill most sensitive to the age-window term of the priority formula (`../priority-engine.md` §5) and the one where a missed window has lifelong consequences.

Safety weight: 0.3. Window: the only MVP skill with a hard-closing developmental window.

## What the research session must produce

1. The exposure categories and a checklist structure (the measurement is coverage and the dog's response quality, not success proportion), including vaccination-schedule-aware guidance for outdoor exposure.
2. How to score a trial (relaxed / neutral / worried / fearful) and how "worried or fearful" triggers distance increase and the fear-period protocol rather than more exposure.
3. The window multiplier curve grounded in sources (replace the v0 breakpoints in `../priority-engine.md` §5).
4. Fear-period guidance (first ~8–11 weeks, secondary ~6–14 months) and how the engine should freeze pushes during them.
5. Adult-dog version: remedial socialization at a slower pace, and the line beyond which it is reactivity and escalates (D-004).
6. Cited sources.
