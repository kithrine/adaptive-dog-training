# Loose-Leash Walking

Status: **v0** founder draft. Measurement: cue progression (proportion of steps or intervals with slack leash). Category: foundation. Safety weight: 0.5. Minimum age: 8 weeks (equipment comfort), outdoor stages after vaccination schedule per vet guidance.

## Principles

- One of the most complex skills: equipment comfort, impulse control, environmental generalization, sustained attention.
- Do not progress to outdoor stages before indoor stages are solid.
- Forward movement is the reward; pulling stops movement. Check-ins and eye contact are rewarded.

## Stages

| # | Name | Distance | Duration | Distraction | Environment | Advance when |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Equipment comfort | stationary | 2–3 min | none | indoor | no resistance to equipment on/off across 3 sessions |
| 2 | Indoor following | room length | 3–5 min | none | familiar room | 80% loose leash across 3 sessions |
| 3 | Outdoor low distraction | yard / driveway | 5 min | low | quiet outdoor | ≥ 50% loose-leash steps in calm outdoor setting |
| 4 | Neighborhood walking | full walk | 10–15 min | medium | familiar route | 80% loose leash on familiar route |
| 5 | High-distraction environments | full walk | 15–20 min | high | parks, pet-friendly stores, other dogs | 80% loose leash in 3 different high-distraction environments |

## Milestones

| Milestone | Stage | Confidence required |
|---|---|---|
| Happy in harness | 1 | 70 |
| Indoor loose leash | 2 | 75 |
| Neighborhood walk | 4 | 75 |
| Public-places walk | 5 | 80 |

## Exercise template (Stage 2 example)

- Parameters: environment indoor, duration 3–5 min, reps n/a (interval-based).
- Setup: leash on, treats in the hand on the dog's side, familiar room.
- Steps: start walking; mark and reward every few steps of slack leash; stop the instant the leash tightens; wait for slack or a check-in; mark, reward, resume.
- Success: leash slack for ~80% of the session; dog checks in without being asked.
- Common mistakes: letting pulling move you forward even once; treats on the far side; sessions too long.
- If struggling: shorten session; reward more often; reduce room size.

## Coaching guidance

| Problem | Response |
|---|---|
| Pulling toward something outdoors | Stop, wait, reward slack; use environmental rewards (allow sniffing as reward); reduce distraction |
| Excited at start of walk | Pre-walk sit and settle; begin with 30 seconds of indoor following |
| Great indoors, chaos outdoors | Expected (PM-012): reset to Stage 3 criteria |
| Owner execution check | Stopping every time? Reward position at the hip? Leash length consistent? |
