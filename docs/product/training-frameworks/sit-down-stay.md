# Basic Obedience: Sit, Down, Stay

Status: **v0** founder draft. Measurement: cue progression. Category: foundation. Safety weight: 0.1 (sit/down), 0.3 (stay). Minimum age: 8 weeks.

Sit and Down share one 4-stage template. Stay is built on top of a fluent sit or down and is the hardest of the three: it requires all three D's in sequence (Duration → Distance → Distraction).

## Principles

- Lure to position in acquisition; fade the lure before adding the verbal cue; cue once.
- Ping-pong the active D (PM-013) so the dog learns the concept, not the pattern.
- Release cue is taught from Stage 2 onward; the behavior ends on the release, not when the dog decides.

## Stages (Sit / Down)

| # | Name | Distance | Duration | Distraction | Environment | Advance when |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Acquisition | 0 ft | < 1 s | none | one location | 50% on verbal cue alone (no lure) |
| 2 | Fluency (duration) | 0 ft | 3–10 s, ping-pong | none | same room | 80% on cue, holding 5+ s |
| 3 | Distance | 1 step → 6 ft, duration reset to 1–3 s | 1–5 s | none | same room | 80% at 6 ft with 5 s |
| 4 | Distraction + generalization (Stay) | variable | variable | low → high | different rooms, yard, friend's house | 80% in 3 environments with mild distraction |

Stage 4 is where "stay" becomes a named skill for the user; internally it is stages 2–4 of sit/down with duration/distance/distraction criteria.

## Milestones

| Milestone | Stage | Confidence required |
|---|---|---|
| Sit on cue | 1→2 | 70 |
| Sit-stay 10 s | 2 | 75 |
| Sit-stay at distance | 3 | 75 |
| Stay with distractions | 4 | 80 |

## Exercise template (Stage 2 example)

- Parameters: reps 8–10, duration ping-pong 2/5/3/8 s, environment same room.
- Setup: quiet room, small treats, dog standing.
- Steps: cue sit once; count silently; mark and reward while the dog is still sitting; release; repeat varying duration.
- Success: 8 of 10 holds to the planned duration.
- Common mistakes: rewarding after the dog gets up; always increasing duration; repeating the cue.
- If struggling: shorten durations to 1–2 s for a session; reward more frequently during the hold.

## Coaching guidance

| Problem | Response |
|---|---|
| Breaks before release | Reward during the hold more often; shorten duration; teach release explicitly |
| Only sits with lure | Fade lure over 3–5 reps; hand signal then verbal |
| Works in kitchen only | Generalization: reset D's in each new room (PM-012) |
| Owner execution check | Cue once? Marking during the behavior? Release used consistently? |
