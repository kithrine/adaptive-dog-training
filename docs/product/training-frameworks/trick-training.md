# Trick Training

Status: **scope brief** for the research session (D-030). No stage data yet.

## Scope

Engagement category. Tricks exist to keep owners and dogs training for fun after foundations, to teach owners shaping and luring mechanics, and to provide "new goal" content for long-term retention. Low safety weight (0.1). No age minimum beyond physical safety (no jumping tricks before growth plates close, ~12–18 months depending on breed).

## What the research session must produce

1. A starter set of 6–10 tricks appropriate for MVP (e.g., touch/hand target, spin, shake, high five, roll over, place/go to mat, leave it as a game), each as a small progression tree (lure → fade lure → cue → duration/distance where relevant).
2. Prerequisites per trick (which foundation stage must be reached first).
3. Physical safety gates (age, size, joint-stress tricks excluded or age-gated).
4. Exercise templates in the standard structure.
5. Coaching guidance for shaping mechanics (rate of reinforcement, criteria splitting), since tricks are where owners learn to train.
6. Cited sources.
