# Jumping Up (Greeting Manners)

Status: **v0** founder draft. Measurement: management track (reduction in jumping frequency; spontaneous alternative behavior rate). Category: common challenge. Safety weight: 0.3 (higher with small children or elderly in household). Minimum age: 8 weeks.

## Principles

- Jumping is reinforced by attention. Everyone in the household and every visitor must follow the same protocol; one inconsistent reward resets progress.
- Teach an incompatible behavior (sit for greeting) rather than only removing reinforcement.
- Manage high-excitement greetings with a leash until the behavior is solid.

## Stages

| # | Name | Distraction | Advance when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Extinction | familiar people | jumping attempts reduced ≥ 50% from baseline over 1 week |
| 2 | Alternative behavior | familiar people + visitors | dog offers sit spontaneously at ≥ 70% of greetings |
| 3 | Generalization | strangers, children, excited visitors | spontaneous sit at ≥ 80% of greetings across 10 different people |

Baseline: jumps per greeting recorded in the first 3 days (the agenda includes a "count greetings" item).

## Milestones

| Milestone | Stage | Confidence required |
|---|---|---|
| Four on the floor at home | 1 | 70 |
| Sit to greet family | 2 | 75 |
| Polite with visitors and strangers | 3 | 80 |

## Exercise template (Stage 2 example)

- Parameters: reps 5–8 greetings, environment home entry, distraction familiar person.
- Setup: helper outside the door; dog on leash or behind a gate; treats ready.
- Steps: helper enters calmly; cue sit; helper approaches only while the dog sits; mark and reward; if the dog jumps, helper turns away and steps back; repeat.
- Success: dog sits on or before approach in 70% of reps.
- Common mistakes: pushing the dog off (attention); greeting with excitement; inconsistent rules among family members.
- If struggling: increase distance between dog and door; lower helper's energy; reward faster.

## Coaching guidance

| Problem | Response |
|---|---|
| Regression when new people visit | Expected regression trigger; temporarily increase management (leash, gate), rehearse calm greeting first |
| Kids get jumped on | Household protocol lesson; kids practice "be a tree"; dog leashed around kids until Stage 2 |
| Owner execution check | Is anyone still rewarding jumping with attention or pushing? Treats ready before the door opens? |
